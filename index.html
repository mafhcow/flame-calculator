How the Expected Gain & Cost Metric Works

Traditional flame calculators estimate the cost to achieve a fixed threshold using a geometric distribution (i.e. stop when reaching ≥ target). However, in actual progression, players often want to know: "Given the flame I have right now, how much expected damage gain will 1 more Eternal Flame provide, and what is the expected cost to gain 1 additional flame score?"

  • Marginal Expected Gain: E[Gain] = Σ P(s) × (s − current) sums the positive surplus score over all possible flame rolls that beat your current item.
  • Expected Flames per +1 Score: 1 / E[Gain] gives the average number of Eternal Flames consumed to improve your equipment's flame score by 1 point.
  • Diminishing Returns: As your current flame reaches higher percentiles (e.g. 95th or 99th percentile), E[Gain] drops sharply and the cost per additional score point escalates exponentially. Use this curve to decide when to stop flaming and invest in other gear pieces!
